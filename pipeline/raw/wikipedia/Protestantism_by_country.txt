== Methodology ==
{{See also|List of Christian denominations}}
For the purposes of this list, the following Christian branches are considered Protestant:

* [[Seventh-day Adventists]]
* [[Anabaptists]] (including [[Amish]], [[Hutterites]], [[Mennonites]])
* [[Anglicans]] (including [[Anglicanism|Episcopalians]])
* [[Baptists]]
* [[Calvinism|Calvinists (or the Reformed tradition)]] (including [[Congregationalists]], [[Presbyterians]], [[Continental Reformed]], [[Waldensians]])
* [[Lutheranism|Lutherans]]
* [[Methodists]]
* [[Pentecostals]]
* [[List of Christian denominations#Protestant|Other Protestants]] (including [[Hussites]], [[Plymouth Brethren|Brethren]], [[Free Evangelical Churches|Free Evangelicals]], [[Quakers]], [[United and uniting churches|United]], [[Holiness movement|Holiness]] and others)

[[Evangelicals]], [[Charismatic movement|Charismatics]], [[Neo-charismatic churches|Neo-charismatics]] and other [[Christian revival|revivalists]] are found virtually across every Protestant branch. [[Nondenominational Christianity|Nondenominationals]], various independents and Protestants from other denominations, not easily fitting in the traditional classification, are also taken into account.

Estimates of total Protestant population vary considerably. Most reliable sources claim a range of 800 million to more than 1 billion. Difficulties occur as there is no consensus among scholars which denominations should be considered Protestant.
